📽️ HiTVideo: Hierarchical Tokenizers for Text-to-video Generation

Ziqin Zhou, et al.

📄 Paper 💻 Project

🎬 Project Overview

HiTVideo introduces a novel hierarchical tokenizer for text-to-video generation, leveraging multi-scale discrete tokens to enhance video generation quality. This work was presented at CVPR 2024.
